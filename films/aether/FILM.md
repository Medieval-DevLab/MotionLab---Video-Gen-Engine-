# Aether: the AI-powered supply chain control tower

## 1. Brief
- **Script:** owner-supplied; quoted line by line in §4.
- **Voiceover:** none for this test, so the film is **timed as if narrated** at about 2.5 words/s with a 0.6 s hold.
  When the owner's VO arrives, the segment times come from it instead.
- **Purpose:** the first film made by the rebuilt engine, a portfolio-grade B2B promo.
- **Brand:** Aether has no brand assets, so a typeset wordmark. Primary colour: Accenture Purple.
- **Length and aspect:** 81.0 s, 16:9, 30 fps (2,430 frames).

## 2. Film system (first film; nothing to differ from yet)
| Dimension | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Style | `journey` | A disruption-to-recovery story is a journey |

| Ground | V2 (sparse depth), arc lower right | Owner's choice |
| Signature device | The journey line: port → hub → decision → execution → close | Connects every act |

| Motion character | Measured | Calm ANZ energy |
| Pacing | 0.6 s hold after lines | Measured |
| Type | Inter | Default |
| Music | `uplifting-inspiring.mp3` (owner-supplied) | Warm, hopeful; matches the calm |
