How to use this

  • Every segment shows its script lines with the voiceover times, then panels from Start → Peak (some have a Build panel in between). Every scene opens sparse and builds, per your note on the Aether port.
  • Below the panels: the text flow, what the visual does, the one primary mover, the move into the next segment, and the sound.
  • Tune any segment with the chips: energy, element count, layout, and sometimes a whole alternative concept. Anything you don't touch stays as planned. Add a note anywhere.
  • When you're done, press Copy my choices and paste the text into our chat. Your choices are saved in this browser if you close the page.

Reading the panels

  • Panels are wireframes at the real 1920×1080 grid and type sizes. The look is final in spirit, not in finish: real style frames come after you approve the plan.
  • Blue dashed arrows and numbers show motion and order. They are annotations, not part of the film.
  • Purple words are the spoken keywords. All on-screen text is the script's own words; any trim is flagged in orange.
  • The Aether part (act 4) uses the journey look you approved, because it is the film MotionLab made. The rest uses a new MotionLab look (see Film-wide choices).
