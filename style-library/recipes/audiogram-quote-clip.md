# Audiogram quote clip

![Audiogram quote clip](../stills/audiogram-quote-clip.jpg)

**A clip from a talk or podcast shown as a quote card: portrait, live waveform and captions lighting word by word** · Fit for our films: **Ready: our kit builds it today** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#audiogram-quote-clip`](../demos/demo.html#audiogram-quote-clip)

## The look
Warm sand (#F2E6D8) with deep ink (#1D1B26) and one accent (#E2583E) for the word being spoken; a duotone portrait in a rounded frame; a thin waveform; captions in a bold grotesque, three lines at most.

## How it moves
Captions light word by word on the speaker's timing, the waveform breathes with the audio, the portrait pushes in very slowly, and the show title sits still at the top.

## Shape of a piece
Show title and guest, the quote plays with captions, the key phrase enlarges, the link to the full episode.

## Craft rules
Captions use the speaker's exact words, three lines at most; the active word is the only coloured text.

## Ask for, before building
The audio clip and transcript timings, guest photo and name, show title

## Where it fits
Podcast promotion, executive quote clip, webinar highlight, event recap

## Build it with our kit
kinetic text word-by-word, real photos, music+SFX, living ground waves
