# Broadcast news package

![Broadcast news package](../stills/broadcast-news-package.jpg)

**Newsroom-style graphics: lower-third straps, a live clock, a ticker, map insets and fact cards over real footage** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#broadcast-news-package`](../demos/demo.html#broadcast-news-package)

## The look
Deep navy (#0B1E3A) with broadcast red (#D0021B) and white; a sturdy condensed sans for straps, a grotesque for body; glossy bar shapes, a corner clock and a ticker strip; real photos and footage full-frame behind.

## How it moves
Straps wipe in from the left in two layers, the ticker crawls the whole time, map insets zoom from region to place, full-screen facts slide up as cards; cuts follow the narration.

## Shape of a piece
The opening sting, a headline strap over footage, two or three fact cards with a map inset, a quote card, the sign-off strap.

## Craft rules
Straps carry the script's own words and stay up long enough to read; the clock and ticker never change position.

## Ask for, before building
Footage or photos, headline and straps, places for maps, quotes with names

## Where it fits
Company news update, internal announcement, market briefing, press-style launch

## Build it with our kit
real photos, kinetic text mask wipe, data notes, camera push, living ground sweeps, music+SFX

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A lower-third strap and ticker crawl component
