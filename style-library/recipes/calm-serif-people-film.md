# Calm serif people film

![Calm serif people film](../stills/calm-serif-people-film.jpg)

**A grown-up, reassuring people film: deep green and gold, a fine serif, duotone portraits and plain promises** · Fit for our films: **Ready: our kit builds it today** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#calm-serif-people-film`](../demos/demo.html#calm-serif-people-film)

## The look
Deep forest green (#0F3B2E) with muted gold (#C8A24B) and warm ivory (#F4EEDF); a high-contrast serif for headlines; portraits in green-gold duotone; thin gold rules and generous margins.

## How it moves
A slow push on each portrait; lines of text rise one at a time and hold; gold rules draw between sections; transitions are soft dissolves through the green.

## Shape of a piece
The reassurance line, three plain promises (hours, support, growth), a portrait with a short quote, how to apply, name and contact.

## Craft rules
Calm pace and short, concrete lines, one promise on screen at a time; real people only.

## Ask for, before building
Real staff portraits, 3 concrete promises, contact channel, tone (formal or warm)

## Where it fits
Recruitment film, employer brand, people or culture story, client trust piece

## Build it with our kit
real photos (duotone), kinetic text rise, hand-drawn strokes (rules), camera push, music

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A duotone filter for photos
