# Continuous-take launch film

![Continuous-take launch film](../stills/continuous-take.jpg)

**No cuts: floods, irises, pills and glass panels grow each scene out of the last** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#continuous-take`](../demos/demo.html#continuous-take)

## The look
Off-white canvas with black UI, or a dark world shown inside a shape. Frosted glass panels over photos, a single accent, real photos inside the design.

## How it moves
No cuts or crossfades. A dot opens like an iris onto the next world, a button floods the frame and contracts into the next object, blobs split and merge with a goo effect. Each morph glides about 0.8 s on one strong in-out curve that the camera zoom shares. One gesture per scene.

## Shape of a piece
Wordmark collapses into its period, the period opens, the ask is typed, a result builds, proof (calendar, chart or notifications), a call-to-action button, and the scene closes back into the dot.

## Craft rules
See techniques 3–4. Never zoom into an off-centre dot; open it instead. A handoff object morphs inside the camera transform of the scene it leaves.

## Ask for, before building
Wordmark, one-line job the product does, three outcomes, aligned day/night or before/after photos.

## Where it fits
Product launches, "how it works in one breath" openers, event openers.

## Build it with our kit
continuity moves, camera, kinetic text mask wipes, screencast UI, real photos.

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** Flood/iris helper (area-eased), goo split/merge filter (blur plus alpha threshold), glass panel (pre-blurred copy of the backdrop, clipped, tinted, rim-lit).
