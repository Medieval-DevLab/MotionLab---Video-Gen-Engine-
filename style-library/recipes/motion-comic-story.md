# Motion-comic story

![Motion-comic story](../stills/motion-comic-story.jpg)

**A graphic-novel story where each panel comes alive: layered depth, one moving element, and frame-breaking peaks** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#motion-comic-story`](../demos/demo.html#motion-comic-story)

## The look
Inked line art (#141414) with flat colour (#2E3A59, #E9DCC9, #D94F30), dramatic angles and caption or speech boxes; light screentone in shadows; bold hand lettering.

## How it moves
Inside each panel the layers separate for parallax and one element truly moves (rain falls, a rope drops, a door opens); the camera travels panel to panel and sometimes breaks the frame for impact.

## Shape of a piece
An establishing panel, the character's want, a tense middle over 2-3 panels, the frame-breaking moment, the resolution panel.

## Craft rules
One moving element per panel; boxes never cover faces; cut on the page turn, not mid-panel.

## Ask for, before building
Script, panel beats, characters, any drawn frames or style references

## Where it fits
Customer story with drama, product origin story, recruitment story, internal change story

## Build it with our kit
camera travel and parallax, kinetic text, shader halftone, hand-drawn strokes, music+SFX

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A panel layout with gutters; a panel-break mask

## Our director prompt
Direct this film (or these segments) in the **Motion-comic story** style. Follow the script word for word, time everything to the voiceover clock, and show me the storyboard before building.

- **Look:** Inked line art (#141414) with flat colour (#2E3A59, #E9DCC9, #D94F30), dramatic angles and caption or speech boxes; light screentone in shadows; bold hand lettering.
- **Motion:** Inside each panel the layers separate for parallax and one element truly moves (rain falls, a rope drops, a door opens); the camera travels panel to panel and sometimes breaks the frame for impact.
- **Shape of the piece:** An establishing panel, the character's want, a tense middle over 2-3 panels, the frame-breaking moment, the resolution panel.
- **Rules:** One moving element per panel; boxes never cover faces; cut on the page turn, not mid-panel.
- **Ask me first for:** script, panel beats, characters, any drawn frames or style references
- **Tuning scale:** Energy +1, Pace 0, Camera +2, Density +1, Background 0, Type +1, Realism −1, Sound +1.
- **Build it with:** camera travel and parallax, kinetic text, shader halftone, hand-drawn strokes, music+SFX. Keep to the engine's craft rules: one primary mover, scenes that open sparse and build, text that is readable before anything ornamental moves.
