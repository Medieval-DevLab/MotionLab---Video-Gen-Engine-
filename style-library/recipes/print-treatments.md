# Painterly and print-culture treatments

![Painterly and print-culture treatments](../stills/print-treatments.jpg)

**Ink wash, risograph, halftone/manga, shadow puppets, oscilloscope, vase painting** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#print-treatments`](../demos/demo.html#print-treatments)

## The look
Ink wash with paper fibre and dry brush; risograph two-ink offset; halftone dots and manga screentone; shadow-puppet silhouettes with jointed limbs behind a warm backlight; an oscilloscope trace; motifs painted around a vase.

## How it moves
Brush reveals, ink blooms that swallow the page, halftone fields that respond to a moving object, joint-driven puppet poses, a print offset on impact.

## Shape of a piece
Used as a segment treatment, not a whole film. It works for one story beat or a cultural chapter.

## Craft rules
Commit to the medium's limits (two inks, one brush, silhouettes only). Keep text clean on top of it.

## Ask for, before building
Which medium, and why it suits the line.

## Where it fits
Regional or cultural campaigns, heritage stories, a chapter break in a long film.

## Build it with our kit
halftone and grain shaders, hand-drawn strokes, living ground.

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A two-ink risograph palette with misregistration; an ink-bloom mask; jointed silhouette parts.
