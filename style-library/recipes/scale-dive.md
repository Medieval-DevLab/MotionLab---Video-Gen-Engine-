# Scale dive

![Scale dive](../stills/scale-dive.jpg)

**One unbroken zoom from room to device to chip to atom, or from street to cosmos** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#scale-dive`](../demos/demo.html#scale-dive)

## The look
Each level is its own clean world (room, device, board, chip, transistor, atom) with consistent line language.

## How it moves
One continuous zoom. Each next world is drawn inside an object of the current one, and the zoom runs in log space so speed feels constant. The inner world zooms less than its frame, which reads as depth.

## Shape of a piece
Five to seven stops, each with one label and one number (for example the scale factor).

## Craft rules
Never zoom into an off-centre point (it swings). Re-centre or open the object instead. Hand off at a fixed on-screen size.

## Ask for, before building
The levels and one fact per level.

## Where it fits
Infrastructure and hardware stories, "from your desk to our data centre", deep-tech explainers.

## Build it with our kit
camera zoom, isometric, data labels.

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A nested-scene zoom helper (a stack of levels, each mounted at a scale and a point inside its parent).
