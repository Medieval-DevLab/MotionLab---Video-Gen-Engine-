# Speaker-ring carousel

![Speaker-ring carousel](../stills/speaker-ring-carousel.jpg)

**Portraits of speakers or panellists sit on a slowly turning ring; the active one comes forward with name and role** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#speaker-ring-carousel`](../demos/demo.html#speaker-ring-carousel)

## The look
Dark plum (#1E1530) with round portrait frames rimmed in a warm accent (#FF8A5B), a soft spotlight behind the active one, the name in a bold grotesque and the role in a light weight.

## How it moves
The ring turns continuously; on each cue the active portrait scales up to the front while the rest dim and soften; name tags slide out from the frame edge and tuck away as it moves on.

## Shape of a piece
The ring forms, each speaker comes forward in turn with name and role, the full ring with the event or show title.

## Craft rules
One face forward at a time, and names stay up long enough to read twice.

## Ask for, before building
Speaker photos, names and roles, show or event title, brand accent

## Where it fits
Webinar and panel intro, podcast opener, event speaker reveal, leadership team intro

## Build it with our kit
real photos, camera with parallax, kinetic text rise, living ground rings, music+SFX

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A ring layout helper (positions on an ellipse with depth scaling)
