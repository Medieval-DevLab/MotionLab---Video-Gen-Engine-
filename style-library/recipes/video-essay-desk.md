# Video-essay paper desk

![Video-essay paper desk](../stills/video-essay-desk.jpg)

**Explainer in video-essay style: printed clippings, highlighter marks, typewriter-tape labels and hand-drawn arrows on a desk** · Fit for our films: **Adapt: needs a small helper or a stylisation** · Live demo: [`demos/demo.html#video-essay-desk`](../demos/demo.html#video-essay-desk)

## The look
Off-white paper scraps on a grey-brown desk (#8E8478), black print, yellow highlighter (#FFE14D) and red pen (#D7263D); typewriter labels on black tape; photos in torn-edge frames; a slight shadow under each scrap.

## How it moves
Clippings slide in and stack, the highlighter swipes across key words, arrows and rings draw on, and the camera pans and pushes over the desk like a document camera.

## Shape of a piece
The question as a headline clipping, evidence pieces one by one, the connecting arrows, the answer highlighted, sources.

## Craft rules
Only real, attributed sources on screen, one highlighted phrase per scrap; keep the desk tidy enough to read.

## Ask for, before building
Sources and quotes, photos, the central question, the conclusion

## Where it fits
Thought-leadership explainer, market or policy briefing, podcast or talk companion

## Build it with our kit
real photos, hand-drawn strokes and handwriting, kinetic text mask wipe (highlighter), camera travel, shader grain

**Small helpers it would want (none built yet; the engine is unchanged):** A torn-edge photo frame; a highlighter swipe mask
